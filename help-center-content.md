## 1. Instructions Guide

### Before you record

- Mount your phone on the head rig. It should sit firmly — if it shakes / shifts while you move, the recording isn't usable.
- Keep the camera lens unobstructed.
- Pick a well-lit space. Natural daylight or bright indoor lighting both work.
- Close all other apps. Background apps can interrupt recording.
- Pick a task from the **Tasks** tab that matches what you're about to do.

### Starting a recording

1. Open the task and tap **Start Recording**. The screen rotates to landscape and locks.
2. Bring both hands into the camera frame and hold them there. The progress ring fills as your hands are detected — once it completes, recording starts automatically and you'll hear _"Recording started."_
3. You can also tap **Skip** under the record button to start manually.

### While recording

- Keep your hands in frame as much as possible.
- Follow the per-task instructions on the task screen (gaze direction, pacing, what to focus on).
- The screen dims to save battery and reduce heat. This is normal.
- Minimum length per clip is **60 seconds**. Anything shorter is discarded.

### Stopping a recording

- Tap the stop button. You'll hear _"Recording stopped"_ and see your contribution time added.
- You stay on the recording screen — tap record again to start a new clip of the same task.
- To switch tasks, exit the recording screen and pick a different task.

### Your first recording (Practice)

Your first run-through is a 60-second practice. It doesn't upload, doesn't appear in History, and doesn't count toward your contribution. After that, every recording counts.

### Uploads

- Recordings upload automatically once they finish.
- Track progress from the **Pending uploads** tile on Home.
- Uploads continue when the app is in the background.

### Payouts

Payments are processed offline. Your earnings will start reflecting in the app soon. Keep recording — your data is safe and your payouts are guaranteed.

## 2. FAQs

**What kind of phone do I need?**
A phone with a 1080p ultrawide rear camera (≥ 110° field of view), gyroscope and accelerometer sensors, and steady performance under load. We run a one-time compatibility check on first launch — if your phone passes, you're good.

**Why did my phone fail the compatibility check?**
The check looks at camera resolution, ultrawide field of view, sensor availability and sustained sample rate, microphone, and whether your device is rooted. The screen lists exactly which checks failed.

**Why did the check re-run on my phone?**
The compatibility check re-runs after every app update or OS update, and the first time you sign in on a new device. This catches changes that could affect capture quality.

**Can I use someone else's phone to record?**
Yes. As long as the phone passes the compatibility check and you sign in with your own Google account, you can use it.

**Can I record children?**
No. By signing up you confirm everyone you record is 18 or older. Recordings of minors are not permitted.

**Can I record indoors and outdoors?**
Yes. Match your task to the setting — some tasks are naturally indoor (cooking, cleaning, laundry); others fit either.

**Why does the app need my location?**
Approximate location helps us understand the diversity of recordings in our dataset. We use coarse location only — never precise GPS.

**How do I get paid?**
Payouts run offline initially. Don't worry, your contributions are being tracked — we'll notify you when payouts start reflecting in the app.

**How long can a recording be?**
Minimum 60 seconds. There's no maximum limit. We'd like you to record the relevant tasks for as long as you can.

**What is the practice recording for?**
It's a one-time, 60-second run-through to get you familiar with the recording flow. It doesn't upload and doesn't count toward your contribution.

**Can I delete a recording?**
No. Once recorded, clips stay in your History and upload automatically.

**Can I cancel an upload?**
No. Uploads run automatically and resume after interruptions. You can see progress from the **Pending uploads** tile on Home.

**Can I watch my recordings?**
Yes — tap a thumbnail in History to play it back. Once a clip has been securely uploaded and the local copy is cleared, the thumbnail stays but playback is no longer available.

**Does the app use mobile data?**
Yes by default. Uploads work over both Wi-Fi and cellular.

**Why did my recording stop on its own?**
A few things can stop a recording mid-flight:

- The phone rotated out of landscape
- An incoming call was answered, or an alarm went off
- The phone got too warm
- The battery dropped to 5% or below

The app tells you what happened with a toast or voice cue.

**Why can't I find a recording I just made?**
If it stopped at less than 60 seconds, it was discarded. Otherwise check the **History** tab — clips appear there even while uploads are still in progress.

**How do I delete my account?**
Profile → Delete account, then type **DELETE** to confirm. Your account is deactivated for **30 days** — log in within that window to restore it. After 30 days, deletion is permanent. Recordings already uploaded remain on our servers.

**What happens if I uninstall the app?**
Local recordings that haven't uploaded yet are lost. Uploaded recordings stay on our servers and your account is unaffected.

**I requested a new task — when will it appear?**
Task requests are reviewed by our team. We'll get back to you as soon as we can.

## 3. Troubleshooting

**Recording won't start**

- _"Phone is too warm"_ — let your phone cool for a few minutes. Remove tight cases that trap heat.
- Battery is below 5% — plug in to charge. New recordings are allowed once you're back above 15%.
- Hand-detection gate isn't passing — see below.

**The hand-detection gate keeps resetting**

- Both hands must be visible at the same time for about 2 seconds.
- Move toward a light source — low light makes hand detection unreliable.
- Remove gloves or anything covering your hands.
- Tap on **Skip** below the recording button to start recording manually.

**Camera preview is black**

- Check that camera permission is granted. Settings → Apps → Humyn Labs → Permissions.
- Close any other app using the camera (video calls, other camera apps).
- Restart the app.

**Recording stopped mid-session**

- Phone rotated out of landscape — keep it locked in landscape on the rig.
- Incoming call was answered, or an alarm fired — these can't be suppressed.
- Phone overheated — let it cool, then try again.
- Battery dropped to 5% — charge before continuing.

**"Recording too short — discarded"**
Clips under 60 seconds aren't saved. Aim for at least a minute per task.

**Compatibility check failed**
The screen lists which checks failed. Most failures are hardware-related and can't be fixed in software. If you're using a borrowed phone, try a different qualifying device. Make sure the phone isn't rooted and was installed from the Play Store.

**Sign-in failed**

- Check your internet connection.
- The app blocks rooted phones, emulators, and sideloaded builds. If that's the cause, install from the Play Store on a non-rooted phone.
- Try again — Google sign-in occasionally times out.

**Uploads aren't progressing**

- Check your internet connection.
- On Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, or Samsung phones, the OS sometimes pauses background uploads. Allow the battery-optimization exemption when prompted, or simply reopen the app to resume.
- Uploads pause while you're recording. They resume after stop.

**Storage warning at compatibility check**
Free up at least 5 GB before recording. A 10-minute clip is roughly 600 MB.

**Phone heats up while recording**

- Some warming is normal, but persistent overheating ends recordings early.
- Remove tight phone cases and avoid recording in direct sunlight.
- Charging while recording can compound heat — record on battery when possible.

**I switched to a new phone — what do I need to do?**
Sign in with the same Google account. The compatibility check will re-run on the new device. Recordings already uploaded from your old phone stay on our servers; local clips that hadn't uploaded yet are tied to the old install and won't appear on the new device.

**I just deleted my account by mistake**
Log in with the same Google account within 30 days. Your account, profile, and History will be restored. After 30 days, deletion is permanent.
