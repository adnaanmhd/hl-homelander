## Anti-patterns to refuse

- **Do NOT lower the hand-gate quality bar to make it pass** — e.g. don't drop to 1-hand detection, don't slash `minHandDetectionConfidence` below a sane floor, don't make the gate trivially auto-pass. The gate exists to enforce that the user actually has the rig mounted with hands in frame (HAND-03/04, mirrors Figure's pattern).
