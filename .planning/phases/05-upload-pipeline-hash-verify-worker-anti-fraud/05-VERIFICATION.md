### Gaps Summary

Phase 5's full breadth shipped — the Wave-1 cleanup (truth #1) is solidly done, and all the structural pieces of the upload pipeline, the hash-verify worker, the events channel, and the client surfaces exist, compile, and pass their unit tests. But three correctness defects flagged by the code review (CR-01, CR-02, CR-03) — independently confirmed here by reading the code — break the resilience guarantees that are the entire point of the phase goal:

- **CR-01 + CR-02 (the same root concern):** the upload coordinator re-POSTs `/recordings/init` on every re-drain, minting a fresh S3 multipart upload that orphans the already-uploaded parts' ETags; and `/recordings/init` is non-idempotent server-side (`.onConflictDoNothing()` keeps the first `s3UploadId` while the response returns a second). Together: a force-quit / process-kill mid-upload, or any upload that exceeds the 15-min presigned TTL (large file on slow cellular — the documented target market), leaves the recording permanently stuck (`/finalize` → `InvalidPart`/`NoSuchUpload` → treated as transient → infinite retry, never dead-letters, never uploads). This defeats truth #2 ("no whole-file restart on chunk fail" — it's worse: it never completes) and truth #3 ("uploads survive backgrounding and force-quit").

- **CR-03:** `drainNow()` is public and invoked directly off three threads (the FGS HandlerThread, the JobService Thread, and the module's `drainExecutor`) with no mutual exclusion — the "only one drain at a time" invariant the design depends on is unenforced; concurrent `uploadOne()` on the same row compounds CR-01/CR-02 and races the queue writes.

Lower-severity warnings (WR-01 non-atomic `/finalize`, WR-02 TOCTOU on the worker's `qa_status` flip, WR-03 events-outbox mutating problem+json responses, the missing prod `sqs-poller.ts`) reinforce the picture but each has a partial backstop and isn't on its own a phase blocker.

Recommendation: route the three blockers (grouped — CR-01/CR-02 are one fix on two sides, CR-03 is the drain-serialisation fix) plus the WR-01/WR-02 warnings to `/gsd-plan-phase --gaps`; the on-hardware behaviors are genuine human-verification items but several of them (force-quit recovery in particular) can't even be smoke-tested until the CR-01/CR-03 fixes land.
